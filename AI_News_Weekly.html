September 2, 2026

Google Unveils Gemini 3.8 Flash, Raising Stakes in AI Model Competition

Google DeepMind publicly launched Gemini 3.8 Flash on Wednesday, September 2, marking the company's third Flash model release in six weeks. The new model combines improved reasoning and coding capabilities with the same speed and cost efficiency as its predecessor, Gemini 3.7 Flash. Google also introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, a specialized variant optimized for cybersecurity applications, as the company accelerates its monthly model release cadence.

Sales AI Startup Clay Secures $7 Billion Valuation in Funding Round

Clay, an artificial intelligence platform designed for sales and marketing teams, has raised new funding at a $7 billion pre-money valuation, led by Wellington Capital. The valuation represents more than a quadrupling of Clay's value since early 2025, reflecting the explosive growth and investor appetite for enterprise AI solutions. The milestone underscores the acceleration of AI adoption in the business software sector.
