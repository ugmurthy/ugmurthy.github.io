7. Delhi Capitals (8 pts, 10M): DC need 5 wins from 4 games — mathematically impossible to reach 18 points. They'd need 4 wins (16 pts) AND multiple teams above them to collapse. ~2% probability.

8. KKR / MI / LSG: All effectively playing for pride and 2027. KKR (7 pts), MI (6 pts), and LSG (6 pts) have insurmountable NRR deficits and point gaps.
