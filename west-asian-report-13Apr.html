Overview of the West Asian Conflict (March 1, 2026 - April 13, 2026)

The West Asian conflict began with a military operation launched by the United States and Israel against Iran on February 28, 2026. This conflict has led to significant casualties, with at least 2,076 killed in Iran and over 26,500 injured. Negotiations Between the USA and Iran in Pakistan

Pakistan hosted peace talks between the U.S. and Iran from April 11-12, 2026, marking the highest-level meeting since 1979. Despite extensive discussions, no peace deal was reached. U.S. Vice President JD Vance noted that Iran rejected U.S. terms concerning its nuclear program and control of the Strait of Hormuz. Iran expressed confidence that regional contacts would continue despite the lack of an immediate agreement.
