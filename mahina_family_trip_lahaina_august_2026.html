📝 Important Travel Tips

☀️ August Weather: Expect warm days (80-88°F/27-31°C) with occasional afternoon showers. Pack light clothing, swimwear, sunscreen (reef-safe), hats, and light rain jackets.
👶 Kids Essentials: Bring car seats if needed (rentals available but limited for multiple children), child-sized life vests, beach toys, reusable water bottles, and familiar comfort items.
🍽️ Vegetarian Dining: Hawaii has embraced plant-based eating. Look for "poi" (taro starch), fresh tropical fruits, poke bowls with no fish, and vegan versions of traditional dishes. Popular chains include Nanea's Hawaiian Eatery and Duke's Waikiki.
🚗 Transportation: Consider renting a large van or two SUVs for 8 people plus gear. Book early for August peak season. Drive time from Lahaina to airport: ~45 min.
🌿 Post-Wildfire Note: Lahaina is still recovering from the 2023 wildfires. Some areas may be closed. Respect restoration efforts and follow all posted signage. The western beaches and resort areas are fully operational.
💰 Budget Tip: Many beaches are free. Save money by packing lunches/picnics from local groceries. Reserve luaus and popular restaurants in advance (especially for groups).
🏥 Safety: Keep copies of important documents. Know location of nearest medical facilities. Have travel insurance. Children should wear life vests in open water. Watch for rip currents and always supervise kids near water.
