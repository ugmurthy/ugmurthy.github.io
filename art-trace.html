3. **Hair Texture Variation** - While the overall hair mass is good, adding more individual strand definition at the edges (especially the wisps coming loose) would add realism.

4. **Ear Detail** - The ear could use slightly more anatomical detail in the cartilage folds to match the level of detail elsewhere.

5. **Background/Edge Treatment** - Consider softening some edges where the face meets the background to create more atmospheric depth.

### **Actionable Suggestions:**

1. **Study Light Direction** - Make sure all shadows consistently follow one light source. Check if the shadow under the chin matches the lighting on the nose and cheeks.

2. **Practice Gradual Transitions** - Use blending stumps or tissue for smoother skin tones, especially in mid-tone areas.

3. **Add Subtle Details** - Small touches like faint pores, slight color variations (even in grayscale), and fine hair strands can elevate realism.

4. **Consider Paper Grain** - You're working on textured paper which adds character - embrace it by varying your pressure to let some texture show through.
